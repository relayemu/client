# Relay

**Your games. Every Apple screen.**

Relay is a native emulator for iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Apple Silicon Mac.
No ads, no tracking, and no games or console firmware included.

Gameplay is free on iPhone, iPad and Apple TV. Relay Pro unlocks Mac gameplay
and advanced play tools. Local play requires no Relay account.

Your library, saves and play history can follow your devices through your
private iCloud, for free. Enable **Sync game files** to include the game files.
Optional Relay Sync and Sync+ provide EU-hosted storage with a free Relay
account and a paid membership. Browser-to-app Transfer requires Relay Pro
and a free Relay account.

| Systems | Core |
|---|---|
| Game Boy, Game Boy Color, Game Boy Advance | mGBA |
| NES, Super NES, Master System, Game Gear, PC Engine (HuCard), WonderSwan, WonderSwan Color | Mesen 2 |
| Nintendo DS | melonDS |
| PlayStation | PCSX-ReARMed |

Optional RetroAchievements support covers the first eleven systems; PlayStation
achievements are not supported. Import only games and firmware you own and
are entitled to use, through Files, AirDrop, Finder or drag and drop.
