# Code of conduct

Relay is a small project. Be direct, be kind, and remember there is a person
on the other side of every issue.

- Discuss the code, not the person. Disagreement is fine; contempt is not.
- No harassment, discrimination or personal attacks, in any project space.
- Do not ask for, offer or link to copyrighted games, firmware or the means to
  obtain them. Relay plays files you own; the project does not help find
  others.
- Report a problem with someone's behaviour to contact@relayemu.app. The
  maintainers will act, including by removing content or people from project
  spaces.

That is the whole policy.
